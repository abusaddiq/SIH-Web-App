Through health, environment and opportunity, SPAK Innovation Hub runs flagship initiatives that turn ideas into practical, inclusive solutions for communities.

A social-impact initiative focused on improving access to healthcare and promoting the wellbeing of underserved communities, particularly children.

  • Community health awareness
  • Health access
  • Partnerships

SpakRecycle

An environmental innovation initiative designed to promote responsible waste management, recycling and sustainable practices.

  • Waste collection
  • Recycling awareness
  • Environmental education
  • Circular-economy opportunities
  • Community participation

Talynq

An AI-enabled campus talent and micro-work platform designed to connect emerging talents with practical opportunities, skill development and enterprise needs.

  • Talent discovery
  • Micro-work opportunities
  • Digital skills
  • Entrepreneurship
  • SME support
  • Inclusive access to economic opportunities
