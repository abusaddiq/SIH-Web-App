Spak Innovation Hub Ltd/GTE was established and registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in 2024. It is an innovation enabler committed to addressing health, environmental and socioeconomic challenges through inclusive innovation, entrepreneurship and practical solutions.

The Hub was established to serve people at the bottom of the ladder, underserved communities and those excluded from opportunities. It seeks to identify pressing societal issues and develop solutions that improve wellbeing, livelihoods and access to opportunity.

Spak Innovation Hub supports and nurtures startups, provides consulting and training, and offers a conducive coworking environment where creativity can develop and talents can connect, collaborate and create.

Why we exist

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a leading inclusive innovation hub that transforms underserved communities through sustainable solutions, entrepreneurship and accessible opportunities.

Our Mission

To enable inclusive innovation by supporting entrepreneurs, nurturing talents, providing practical training and consulting, and developing solutions to health, environmental and socioeconomic challenges.

What we aim to do

Our Objectives

See flagship projects
  1. 01

    Develop and support innovative solutions to pressing health, environmental and community challenges.

  2. 02

    Identify, nurture and accelerate startups, entrepreneurs and emerging talents, particularly among underserved populations.

  3. 03

    Provide accessible entrepreneurship training, consulting, mentoring and capacity-building opportunities.

  4. 04

    Create a collaborative coworking environment where individuals and organisations can connect, collaborate and create.

  5. 05

    Promote environmental sustainability through recycling, waste reduction and circular-economy initiatives.

  6. 06

    Improve access to opportunities, skills, resources and networks for people who are excluded or underserved.

  7. 07

    Leverage technology and innovation to create economic opportunities and improve quality of life.

  8. 08

    Build partnerships with institutions, communities, businesses and development organisations to scale impactful solutions.

What guides us

Our Core Values

Six values shape every programme, project and partnership at SPAK Innovation Hub.

Inclusion

Promoting equal access to opportunities and prioritising underserved people and communities.

Innovation

Encouraging creative thinking and practical solutions to real-world problems.

Integrity

Acting with honesty, accountability and transparency.

Sustainability

Pursuing solutions that create lasting social, environmental and economic value.

Collaboration

Achieving meaningful impact through partnerships and collective action.

Impact

Focusing on measurable improvements in lives, livelihoods and communities.
