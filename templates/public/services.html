What we do

Innovation, entrepreneurship and practical solutions

As an innovation enabler, SPAK Innovation Hub supports startups, provides consulting and training, and offers spaces where people and organisations can connect, collaborate and create.

Key activities

Where SPAK focuses its work

Six areas of activity drive SPAK's mission to enable inclusive innovation — serving people at the bottom of the ladder, underserved communities and those excluded from opportunities.

Startup Support and Incubation

Supporting aspiring and existing entrepreneurs through idea development, business modelling, mentoring, incubation, acceleration and access to relevant opportunities.

Consulting and Training

Providing practical training and consulting in entrepreneurship, business development, innovation, digital skills, organisational development and related areas.

Coworking and Collaborative Workspace

Providing a conducive environment for creativity, productivity and enterprise development, enabling talents and organisations to connect, collaborate and create.

Social and Environmental Innovation

Identifying community challenges, developing practical interventions and promoting inclusive solutions that improve wellbeing and environmental sustainability.

Talent Development and Opportunity Creation

Nurturing emerging talents, strengthening employability and entrepreneurial capabilities, and connecting individuals to learning, mentorship and work opportunities.

Partnerships and Ecosystem Development

Collaborating with institutions, businesses, communities, development organisations and other stakeholders to expand access to resources and scale impact.
